# Brief & positioning

## What we sell

Custom **generative installations** — real-time visuals on projection, LED and interactive surfaces — for:

- **Conferences** — stage backdrops, sponsor booths, registration zones
- **Events & launches** — touring, audio-reactive brand experiences
- **Offices & lobbies** — permanent, always-on brand walls driven by company data
- **Exhibitions & trade shows** — interactive stands that pull and hold visitors

Delivered **turnkey**: concept → engine → hardware → install → operator → support.

## Who buys

Event/brand/marketing managers, agency producers, workplace & facilities leads. They need to justify spend
to someone else, so the site must give them **business arguments**, not art talk.

## The site's job

Convert visitors into proposal requests (`Get a proposal` → pre-filled email; form + CRM later).

## Core messages (in priority order)

1. **Attention / footfall** — installations stop people; stands get visited; dwell time goes up.
2. **Customised to your brand** — palette, type, shapes, data and interaction are the client's. "Your brand is the input, not a logo in the corner."
3. **Measurable outcomes** — reach, dwell time, leads; every case shows results.
4. **Reuse = lower cost** — one engine across events/formats, no re-rendering video.
5. **Turnkey & reliable** — hardware, install, operator; "opening night is not a beta".

## Copy rules

- Lead with the benefit to the business, then the craft.
- Every section ends with a path to the CTA.
- Concrete over clever: venues, sizes, timelines, numbers.
- Results must be real and verifiable before launch — current numbers are placeholders.
- English only (for now). Short sentences. No jargon like "GLSL", "R3F" in marketing copy.
