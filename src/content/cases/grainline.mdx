## The challenge

A headquarters lobby with a large LED wall that showed the same corporate video for years. The client wanted something alive that reflects the company — and costs nothing to keep fresh

## The installation

A permanent generative lattice that runs 24/7. Internal KPIs (non-sensitive, aggregated) set its rhythm through the day; campaigns switch it into seasonal modes from a simple control panel

<GenerativeWork work="lattice" seed={9} density={0.35} className="my-10 aspect-[4/3] rounded-lg" />

## Built around their brand

- Corporate colours and the brand grid define every cell
- Marketing can switch campaign modes without calling us

## What it delivered

The wall has run for a year without new content production and has been reused for six internal campaigns
