## The challenge

A fintech summit where every sponsor shows the same looped video. The client wanted a main-stage presence that people would film, talk about, and remember — without producing new content for every session

## The installation

A 24-metre LED backdrop running a live generative system. Trading volume from the client's own platform drives density; volatility drives the motion. The stage looks different every minute, yet always unmistakably theirs

<GenerativeWork work="signal-field" seed={11} density={0.55} className="my-10 aspect-[16/9] rounded-lg" />

## Built around their brand

- The palette is the client's brand palette — no off-brand frame is possible
- On the keynote cue, the field resolves into the logo, then dissolves back
- Speakers' names and session titles render in the brand typeface over the live field

## What it delivered

The backdrop became the summit's most-photographed spot. The same engine now runs on the client's booth screens and social loops
