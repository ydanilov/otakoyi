## The challenge

A crowded trade show hall where stands compete for seconds of attention. The client needed people to stop, stay, and leave their details

## The installation

A 30-metre projection floor. Depth cameras track visitors; their positions bend a height map whose contour lines are projected beneath them. The topography slowly remembers where people stood

## Built around their brand

- Contour lines are shaped by the client's logo silhouette
- A branded photo moment captures each visitor's "trail" — with lead capture by QR

## What it delivered

The stand had the longest dwell time on the floor and a queue on the opening day
